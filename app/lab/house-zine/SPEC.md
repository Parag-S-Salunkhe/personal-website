# House Zine (/lab/house-zine)

Idea: the risograph zine Parag hands guests at the door. Two inks on cool paper.

- Palette: paper #F1F1EC, riso blue #2F4FA2 (text ink), riso pink #FF4FA3 (second ink, fills and decoration only), riso yellow #FFE14A (tape, scraps), ink #1B2350 (text on pink/yellow), overprint #5A3E8E (reserved, unused).
- Type: Anybody (wdth 150 / 900 for display, wdth 60 for labels, wdth 100 for titles), Literata for reading, Reenie Beanie for margin scribbles.
- Signature moment: the print run. Pink layer wipes in, then the blue layer slides into near-registration; a 4px/3px pink misregistration stays. Halftone dots sit behind. Taped items settle with a small rotation (opacity never hidden).
- Motif: halftone dots, tape, torn slip edge, scissor cut lines between sections, staples, hand-drawn circles round ratings.
- Cards: movies = pink/blue duotone posters (CSS blend) taped on; dishes = dashed-cut recipe clipping with hand-drawn plate SVG (3 variants); posts = two-page spread with staples; notes = yellow sticky scraps. No-poster state is a clapperboard SVG.
- Light/dark: light only (paper is the point). Dark would need an inverted ink set.
- Traps avoided: not cream/serif/clay (cool paper, fluorescent inks, serif only for reading); no eyebrow caps, no gradient text, no card kit (each type has its own artifact), no coloured side borders, no glass.
- Earned exception: the pink text-shadow on headings is a riso misregistration, not a neo-brutalist shadow.
- Contrast: pink never carries text; blue on paper about 6.6:1, ink on yellow/pink above 5:1.
- With more time: duotone hover reveal to full colour, a print-run replay on scroll for section titles, item pages in the same style, a real photo collage in the hero, a dark "night print" variant.
