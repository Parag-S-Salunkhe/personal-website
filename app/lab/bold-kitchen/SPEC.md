# Bold Kitchen (/lab/bold-kitchen)

**Palette (light):** tile #F6F6F1, grout #E6E6DD, ink #17171C, tomato #E5421E (cooking), cobalt #1E4FA3 (movies), mustard #F2B33D (blog), pea #3E7B4F (notes/projects). Ink text on tomato and mustard; tile text on cobalt and pea.

**Type:** Archivo only, via next/font with the wdth axis. Display is 900 at wdth 125 (giant category words, up to 11rem as a deliberate poster moment); titles 800 at wdth 112; body 450 at wdth 100.

**Signature moment:** the tile wipe. A 6-column grid of enamel colour tiles wipes in with clip-path on a diagonal stagger, then PARAG / SALUN K / HE set themselves letter by letter inside the tiles (the h1 is real text, tiles are aria-hidden). The ink tile beside HE holds the note form. Reduced motion shows the final state at once.

**Motif:** 8px tile grid, enamel mug/pot/half-disc SVGs, pantry labels (category huge, ruled ingredient list), ratings as filled squares, rounded colour-field bands (2rem to 3rem radius, deliberate enamel).

**Cards:** movie = poster (or film-reel SVG) on cobalt with the title huge beneath; dish = pantry label with an enamel pot SVG, since no dish has a photo; post = ink poster block on mustard; note = small tile label.

**Interaction:** each section tile slides its label up to show the latest item on hover-capable devices; on touch the latest line is always visible.

**Light/dark:** light only, colour fields carry the drama. No dark switch.

**Site-design section 3 traps avoided:** not cream+serif+clay (tile white is cool, sans only); not black plus one accent (four enamel colours); not broadsheet (no hairline columns, big radii and fields); not the SaaS card kit (three card types with different radii and fills); no eyebrow labels, no gradient text, no side borders, no numbering, no meta strings.

**With more time:** real dish photos and a hover-to-flip pantry label; per-item pages linked from rows; a reduced-height hero on short laptops; a quieter wipe on slow phones; a hand-drawn mug in place of the generic one.
